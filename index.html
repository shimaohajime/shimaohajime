Home

I am currently an Assistant Professor of Data Analytics at Penn State Great Valley. I obtained my Ph.D. in Economics from Purdue University in 2018 and formerly worked at the Santa Fe Institute and McGill University before joining Penn State.

My current research focuses mainly on bridging artificial intelligence, empirical social science, and theoretical modeling in information systems and economics.
